# Role-Based Paths

AI-ready data is a shared outcome, not a task assigned only to data engineers.
Choose the path closest to your accountability, then test the result with a
different role.

## Data, software, or AI practitioner

**What this unlocks:** information that code, retrieval, models, and automation
can consume with explicit meaning and failure behavior.

1. Read [The AI-Ready Data Journey](AI-READY-DATA-JOURNEY.md).
2. Complete the [Data-Product Contract](data-product-contract.md).
3. Assess one AI use with the
   [AI-Data Readiness Assessment](ai-data-readiness-assessment.md).

Leave with: a named use, authoritative meaning, evidence requirements, and an
abstention rule—not a universal “ready” label.

## Architect or data-product owner

**What this unlocks:** a maintained information promise whose lineage,
ownership, access, and correction path survive team boundaries.

1. Resolve meaning with the
   [Semantic-Authority Record](semantic-authority-record.md).
2. Connect fitness to observable policy in the
   [Quality-Evidence Scorecard](quality-evidence-scorecard.md).
3. Define reconstruction and correction through
   [Provenance Requirements](provenance-requirements.md).

Leave with: a decision boundary between platform capability, information
fitness, and AI use.

## Manager or program owner

**What this unlocks:** a prioritized readiness backlog tied to valuable uses
instead of an endless enterprise cleanup program.

1. Select one high-value, bounded use and one affected decision.
2. Use the [Value and Evidence Ledger](VALUE-AND-EVIDENCE-LEDGER.md) to record
   current delay, rework, uncertainty, or exposure.
3. Fund the smallest meaning, quality, lineage, and operating changes that can
   prove or reject readiness for that use.

Leave with: a sequenced investment and evidence plan.

## Executive or decision owner

**What this unlocks:** a defensible answer to “Can our data support this AI
investment?” without confusing access, volume, or a green pipeline with trust.

1. Read the [Executive Decision Brief](EXECUTIVE-DECISION-BRIEF.md).
2. Require a named use, accountable meaning owner, stop-trust conditions, and
   separate action/release authority.
3. Decide whether to pilot, condition, redesign, defer, or reject.

Leave with: an evidence-bounded investment decision and visible residual risk.

## Cross-role handoff

Ask someone outside the data team to explain the approved use, one known gap,
when the system must abstain, and who fixes a corrected source. If they can
only say “the data passed quality checks,” the case is not yet transferable.
