## A fresh ten-minute transfer task

These are invented facts for an unrelated equipment-maintenance report. They
are not Northbridge history or an observed customer case.

A maintenance lead needs a daily 08:00 list of machines due for inspection.
The approved rule requires every active machine to be represented, including
newly installed units. The asset register lists 80 established machines and
20 new ones. A report contains all 80 established machines and 15 new ones.
Its pipeline is green. The report owner proposes releasing it because it is
95% complete. No narrower-use approval is supplied.

Write a Quality-Evidence Scorecard entry containing the overall percentage,
new-machine percentage, affected use, release disposition, repair owner, and
reopening evidence. Leave any owner not supplied by the facts visibly unassigned.

**Worked response:** Overall coverage is 95/100 = 95%; new-machine coverage is
15/20 = 75%. Neither meets the stated all-active-machine rule. Withhold the
full inspection list, record the five missing machines, and assign a resolver
through the product's ownership process. The scenario does not name that
person, so a name must not be invented. Reopen only after reconciling the full
active population and checking the report against the rule.

**Changed condition:** all five records arrive, but the list has already missed
the 08:00 deadline. Coverage may now pass. The service failure remains: notify
the maintenance lead and determine which inspections need reconsideration.
Record coverage repair and late delivery separately.

## Read-back check

Ask a colleague to explain why the percentage alone did not authorize release,
what changed after repair, and what still needs an owner. Record actual
confusion or unsafe assumptions if a consenting person tries the task. Until
then, actual practitioner sessions for this new exercise are zero.
