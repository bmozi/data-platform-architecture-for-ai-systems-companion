# Value and Evidence Ledger

Use this to connect data-platform work to a named use and decision. It prevents
“clean all the data” from becoming an unbounded program and prevents hoped-for
benefits from becoming claimed results.

## Five-minute first row

| AI or information use | Who benefits | Current friction or exposure | Readiness change | Evidence to collect | Decision it informs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Retrieve current partner policy for an analyst | Analyst and partner | Manual search; superseded policies appear current | Meaning authority, versioned provenance, entitlement, abstention | Citation coverage, superseded-result rate, unresolved cases, review time | Keep human search, pilot retrieval, constrain, or expand |

## Evidence rules

- Label every number **observed**, **reported**, **estimated**, or **unknown**.
- Measure fitness for the named use, not generic “data quality.”
- Include corrections, exclusions, manual review, denied uses, and abstentions.
- A successful pipeline run does not prove semantic correctness or usefulness.
- Do not claim the data caused a business result without an appropriate design
  and named uncertainty.

## Monday-morning use

Choose the proposed AI use with the clearest affected person and highest cost
of wrong information. Capture the current baseline before changing the data or
model. The first valuable result may be a defensible **not ready for this use**.
