# Start Here: One Information Promise in Thirty Minutes

**Reader route:** Complete the thirty-minute exercise output first. The
facilitator-only freeze, manifest, and evidence protocol is a separate route
for controlled pilots; do not open it until the first-pass artifact is done.

Choose one number, dataset, feature set, document source, or retrieval corpus
that matters to a real person or decision. Do not begin with the platform.

## Before you begin

Use this route with *Data Platform Architecture for AI Systems*. Read the
relevant chapter or chapters first: this repository supplies worksheets and
decision moves, while the book supplies the reasoning needed to interpret
tradeoffs and challenge a result. Without the book, treat the output as a
draft hypothesis for discussion, not as a production or release decision.

## 0–5 minutes: name the use

Complete three sentences:

1. This named consumer needs information to ___;
2. a wrong or stale answer could ___;
3. dependable information would unlock ___.

If the consumer is “everyone” or the use is “AI,” narrow the decision.

## 5–15 minutes: define the product

Complete the [five-line first contract](data-product-contract.md#ten-minute-first-contract)
in the Data-Product Contract. Compare it with the
[miniature Northbridge example](data-product-contract.md#miniature-example).

Stop if the meaning owner, time boundary, or stop-trust condition is unknown.

## 15–25 minutes: test one AI use separately

If AI will retrieve, transform, rank, summarize, or act from this information,
complete the [six-line first assessment](ai-data-readiness-assessment.md#ten-minute-first-assessment).
Passing the data question cannot grant tool, agent, workflow, or release
authority.

## 25–30 minutes: ask for a read-back

Give the short contract to someone who neither owns the source nor built the
pipeline. Ask what the information means, who uses it, when it becomes
untrustworthy, and who may change the promise.

## Your first result

You now have a reviewable information-product hypothesis and, if relevant, a
bounded AI-use question—not a universal trust label. Continue with the
[completed contract](examples/northbridge-data-product-contract-v1.md), the
[readiness example](examples/northbridge-ai-data-readiness-assessment-v1.md),
and the semantic, quality, provenance, and access decisions they expose.

For a team decision, use the [Pilot and Usability Route](PILOT-AND-USABILITY.md)
after this first pass. A completed worksheet is not evidence that the route is
usable, the data is fit, or a production release is authorized.

For supplied practice after each chapter, use the [chapter practice route](CHAPTER-PRACTICE.md). It includes an unrelated maintenance-report exercise and worked response.
