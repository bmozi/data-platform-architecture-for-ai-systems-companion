# Data Meaning, Provenance, and Permitted-Use Contract Test Addendum

**State:** `PREPARED/UNRUN`
**Scope:** Additional test route for the signature Contract. This does not
alter, replace, or claim execution of the existing Cedar Vale packet.

## Purpose

Test whether a practitioner can distinguish data availability from decision
fitness and keep data permission separate from downstream action authority.

## Minimum controlled task

Give a participant an unfamiliar product description with a disputed term, a
freshness or cohort limit, an access grant, and a proposed AI-supported use.
Provide the signature Contract and linked canonical blank tools, but not a
completed example.

Ask the participant to:

1. name the consumer, decision, output role, semantic authority, and time
   boundary;
2. distinguish permitted and prohibited purpose, inference, and action;
3. record a stop-trust or revalidation trigger; and
4. identify the separate owner and review required before a downstream action.

## Retained observations

Record task time, omitted decision/use boundaries, mistaken equivalence of
access and purpose, unsupported authority claims, unresolved resolver links,
participant questions, and the participant's explanation of when to stop
trusting the data. Keep exact inputs and anonymized output only with authorized
recruitment and any required consent.

## Failure and stop conditions

Treat the tool as needing revision if a participant can complete the Contract
while leaving an ambiguous meaning, unbounded use, unsupported inference,
unowned action decision, or absent revalidation trigger. Stop the session if
the scenario or participant data exceeds approved scope. A successful
completion does not prove fitness, legal compliance, safety, usability
generally, or business value.
