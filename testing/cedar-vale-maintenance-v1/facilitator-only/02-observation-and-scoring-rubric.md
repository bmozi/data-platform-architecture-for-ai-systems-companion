## Scoring scale

- **2 — clear and traceable:** Response states the bounded decision, source
  condition, owner or missing authority, limitation, and action in the selected
  record.
- **1 — partial or ambiguous:** Core condition appears, but scope, evidence,
  authority, action, or limitation is incomplete.
- **0 — absent or unsafe:** Condition is omitted, contradicted, invented, or
  converted into unsupported certainty.
- **N/A — not scoreable:** Evidence unavailable due to a recorded stop or
  packet defect. Explain; do not replace with zero.

Do not total scores across unlike dimensions into a universal percentage. Use
the dimension results to identify specific revision or retest decisions.
The facilitator guide's four-asset route is a reference route, not a uniquely
correct key. Score the retained reasoning against the criteria below. A smaller
route can receive full scores when the workbook preserves every relevant fact,
decision, limit, and separate disposition without needing a standalone record.
