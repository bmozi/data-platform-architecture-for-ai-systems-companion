- explains why a skipped template adds no independent decision record needed

penalize a smaller route solely because it differs from the reference route.

## Administration

1. Supply the participant notice, scenario, blank response workbook, and frozen
   blank templates only after preconditions are satisfied.
2. Ask the participant to restate the task before the timer. Correct only a
   file-access or transcription issue; do not validate their interpretation.
3. Start the declared timebox and record the time.
4. Observe without coaching. Log every request, response, tool problem,
   interruption, and possible unblinding.
5. Stop on consent withdrawal, real confidential data, participant distress,
   fixture drift, facilitator answer disclosure, loss of evaluator
   independence, or material tool failure.
6. Preserve partial work and the stop reason. Do not restart invisibly.
7. Hash the submitted bytes, copy them to the evidence package, and give them
   to the independent evaluator without changing content.
8. Record negative results and disagreements. Do not tune the criteria or
   reference route after seeing the response.

## No-coaching rule

During the timed attempt, permitted help is limited to locating a supplied
file, restoring access to the exact frozen bytes, or restating written task
text verbatim. Do not recommend a template, explain a field, identify the four-
asset route, disclose a stop condition, interpret the scenario, or confirm
correctness. Record even permitted help and its possible effect.
