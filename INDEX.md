## By role

- **Data engineer:** begin with named consumers, grain, transformations,
  correction, quality, and lineage.
- **Architect or data-product owner:** begin with the information promise,
  semantic authority, service ownership, and change rules.
- **Analyst or operator:** identify the decision, time boundary, cohorts,
  evidence, and stop-trust conditions.
- **Privacy, security, or governance:** identify purpose, entitlement,
  classification, retention, provenance, and prohibited uses.
- **AI builder:** assess one retrieval, training, evaluation, or action use at a
  time and keep data fitness separate from model and action authority.
